A team releases their app on both iOS and Android. They open App Store Connect and see a **Conversion Rate of 3.8%**. They open Google Play Console and see a **Conversion Rate of 28.4%**.

The growth team immediately assumes their Android listing is performing 7x better than their iOS listing.

In reality, **both platforms may be performing identically**.

The apparent 7x difference is an illusion created by fundamentally different metric definitions: **Apple and Google define the top of the conversion funnel in completely incompatible ways.**

In this guide, we break down the exact mathematical definitions used by App Store Connect and Google Play Console, explain why comparing them directly is invalid, and show how to normalize conversion metrics across both platforms.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               CONVERSION FUNNEL DEFINITIONS: APPLE vs GOOGLE                      |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  APPLE APP STORE CONNECT                                                          |
|  [Impressions (Search Scroll, Today Tab, Browse)]  ──► 100,000 Impressions        |
|                            │                                                      |
|                            ▼                                                      |
|  [App Downloads (Direct from Search or Page View)] ──► 3,800 Installs             |
|  --> Calculated Conversion Rate: 3,800 / 100,000 = 3.8% (Impression-to-Install)   |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  GOOGLE PLAY CONSOLE                                                              |
|  [Store Listing Visitors (Users Who Visited the Full Page)] ──► 13,380 Page Views |
|                            │                                                      |
|                            ▼                                                      |
|  [Installers on Store Listing]                     ──► 3,800 Installs             |
|  --> Calculated Conversion Rate: 3,800 / 13,380 = 28.4% (Page-to-Install)         |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## The Core Conflict: Impressions vs Store Listing Visitors

### 1. Apple App Store Connect Definition
In App Store Connect, the conversion rate formula is:

```math
\text{iOS Conversion Rate} = \frac{\text{Total Downloads}}{\text{Unique Device Impressions}}
```

An **Impression** is counted whenever your app icon appears on screen for more than 1 second anywhere in the App Store:
* Scrolled past in search results.
* Featured in a category list or Today tab story.
* Viewed on your full product page.

Because millions of users scroll past search results without tapping, the denominator is massive, yielding conversion rates typically between **2.0% and 6.0%**.

### 2. Google Play Console Definition
In Google Play Console, the primary conversion rate formula is:

```math
\text{Play Store Listing Conversion Rate} = \frac{\text{Installers}}{\text{Store Listing Visitors}}
```

A **Store Listing Visitor** is counted *only when a user explicitly taps into your full product page details*.
* Google Play **excludes** search result impressions from this denominator.

Because users who tap into a full product page have already demonstrated high intent, the denominator is small, yielding conversion rates typically between **20.0% and 45.0%**.

## Full Funnel Comparison Matrix

| Funnel Stage | Apple App Store Connect | Google Play Console | Direct Comparison Valid? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top of Funnel** | **Impressions** (Includes search scroll) | **Search Impressions** (Tracked separately in Search performance) | **NO** (Must use Play Search performance table) |
| **Mid Funnel** | **Product Page Views** (Taps into page) | **Store Listing Visitors** (Page views) | **YES** (Directly comparable metric) |
| **Bottom of Funnel** | **Units** (First-time downloads + Re-downloads) | **Acquisitions** (First-time installers) | **PARTIALLY** (Must separate re-downloads) |
| **Reported CVR** | **Impression $\rightarrow$ Download** (3%–6%) | **Page View $\rightarrow$ Install** (20%–45%) | **NO (Fatal Error)** |

## How to Normalize Conversion Rates Across Stores

To compare your conversion performance accurately across iOS and Android, you must align the denominators:

```
[Normalized Multi-Store Metrics]
1. True Page-to-Install Conversion (Mid-Funnel Efficiency)
   - iOS:   Total Downloads / Product Page Views
   - Play:  Installers / Store Listing Visitors
   --> Benchmark: Both should range between 22% and 38% for healthy utilities.

2. Search-to-Install Conversion (Top-Funnel Efficiency)
   - iOS:   Search Downloads / Search Impressions
   - Play:  Play Search Installers / Play Search Impressions
   --> Benchmark: Both should range between 2.5% and 5.5%.
```

## Practical Takeaways for Your Team

1. **Never Report a Single Combined CVR Metric**: Never average Apple's 3.8% and Google's 28.4% into a single company KPI.
2. **Track Search Conversion Separately from Browse Conversion**: Search conversion measures keyword relevancy; Browse/Explore conversion measures general category appeal.
3. **Audit First-Time vs Re-downloads**: If 35% of your iOS downloads are returning churned users re-downloading the app, your first-time acquisition rate is lower than reported.
