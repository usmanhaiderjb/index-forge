Adding a video preview to your App Store or Google Play listing feels like an obvious win. Video is dynamic, engaging, and modern.

Yet in conversion testing across hundreds of apps, **adding a video preview decreases conversion rates in over 70% of initial A/B tests**.

Why?

Because most developers produce **cinematic brand commercials** instead of **fast, muted UI demonstrations**. On the App Store, videos autoplay on mute in search results. If your video opens with a slow 4-second animated logo fade-in, the user has already scrolled past your listing before seeing a single feature.

In this guide, we break down the rules of App Store Previews vs Google Play Promo Videos, explain why videos fail, and provide the exact 30-second storyboard architecture that lifts conversion.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               APP STORE PREVIEW (iOS) vs GOOGLE PLAY PROMO VIDEO                  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  Dimension                 Apple App Store Preview     Google Play Promo Video    |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  Max Length                30 seconds (Strict)         2 minutes (YouTube Embed)  |
|  Autoplay Behavior         Autoplay MUTED in search    Autoplay on listing page   |
|  Orientation               Portrait or Landscape       Landscape (16:9 YouTube)   |
|  Allowed Content           In-app UI capture ONLY      Live-action + UI + 3D motion|
|  Max Video Count           Up to 3 videos per locale   1 YouTube video link       |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## The 3 Reasons App Store Videos Lower Conversion

### 1. The "Poster Frame" Fallback Trap
On iOS, if a user has Low Power Mode enabled or disabled Video Autoplay in iOS Settings, Apple displays the **Poster Frame** (the single still thumbnail you select during video upload).
* If your poster frame is a blurry transition frame or an empty onboarding screen, visitors see a low-quality static image instead of your high-converting Screenshot #1.

### 2. Slow Pacing & Cinematic Intros
In mobile search, user decision velocity is measured in milliseconds:
* **Second 0.0 – 1.5**: User's thumb is in mid-scroll.
* If the video is black or shows a spinning 3D logo, the user scrolls away.
* A high-converting preview video must show the **core feature in action within the first 1.0 second**.

### 3. Relying on Audio / Voiceovers
Over **85% of App Store searches happen with device audio muted**. If your video relies on a voiceover to explain what is happening, it fails completely. Every action on screen must be reinforced with **large, high-contrast text callout overlays**.

## The Winning 30-Second Storyboard Formula

To produce an App Store Preview that consistently beats a static screenshot set in A/B tests, follow this 4-beat structure:

```
[30-Second High-Conversion Storyboard Architecture]
┌─────────────────┬──────────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────────┐
│ Beat 1 (0–3s)   │ Beat 2 (4–12s)   │ Beat 3 (13–22s) │ Beat 4 (23–30s) │
│ THE CORE HOOK   │ SPEED & WORKFLOW │ SMART FEATURES  │ VALUE & CTA     │
│ Instant feature │ 1-tap solution   │ Pro capabilities│ Clean sign-off  │
│ UI interaction  │ in real-time UI  │ in fast cuts    │ with logo end   │
└─────────────────┴──────────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┘
```

| Timecode | Visual Screen Action | High-Contrast Text Overlay | User Psychology |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **00:00 – 00:03** | Finger taps "Scan Document" $\rightarrow$ Camera auto-detects edges in 0.5s | *"Scan Any Document in 1 Second"* | Instant proof of core speed benefit |

## Technical Specifications & App Review Guidelines

When exporting video assets for App Store Connect, follow these exact encoding parameters:

* **Container**: `.mp4` or `.mov` (H.264 or ProRes 422).
* **Frame Rate**: Exactly 30 fps or 60 fps (do not use 24 fps or 29.97 fps).
* **Audio**: Stereo AAC, 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz.
* **Apple Compliance Rule**: **Only captured in-app UI is permitted.** You cannot show hands holding physical devices, live actors talking to cameras, or desktop interfaces. Apple will reject any preview video containing non-app footage.
