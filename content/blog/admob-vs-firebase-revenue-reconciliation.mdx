## The Root Cause: Telemetry Observers vs Settlement Systems

To understand why AdMob and Firebase disagree, you must distinguish between an **Event Telemetry Observer** and a **Financial Settlement Engine**:

### 1. Google AdMob (The Settlement Engine)
* **What It Measures**: AdMob manages the ad server, conducts real-time programmatic bidding auctions, and pays out revenue to your bank account.
* **Accuracy**: 100% financial ground truth. It factors in invalid traffic deductions, clawbacks, and currency settlement rates.
* **Timing**: Figures are updated on a 24-hour delayed settlement window.

### 2. Firebase / GA4 (The Telemetry Observer)
* **What It Measures**: Firebase listens to the client-side `ad_impression` event fired by the mobile SDK on the user's phone.
* **Accuracy**: An estimate calculated from average eCPM values passed to the device.
* **Discrepancy Triggers**:
  * Users going offline before event dispatch.
  * Ad blockers preventing analytics pings.
  * Invalid ad clicks filtered out by AdMob servers but already logged locally by Firebase.

## The Additive vs Mutually Exclusive Matrix

Not all revenue sources are mutually exclusive. When aggregating revenue across multiple providers, apply this strict precedence matrix:

| Metric Category | Providers Connected | Aggregation Rule | Correct Mathematical Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **In-App Ad Revenue** | AdMob + Firebase GA4 | **Mutually Exclusive** (Source Precedence) | **AdMob wins.** Firebase value is suppressed from total. |
| **Ad Spend / UA** | Google Ads + Apple Search Ads | **Additive** | **Summed.** (Google Ads spend + ASA spend). Different money spent in different networks. |
| **In-App Purchases (IAP)** | App Store Connect + Play Console | **Additive across platforms** | **Summed across OS.** (iOS IAP + Android IAP). |
| **Subscription Proceeds** | StoreKit + Firebase In-App Purchase | **Mutually Exclusive** | **StoreKit / App Store Connect wins.** |

## 3 Rules for Clean Mobile Financial Reporting

1. **Never Calculate eCPM on Client Logs**: Calculate effective CPM as:
   ```
eCPM = (Settled AdMob Revenue / Verified AdMob Impressions) * 1,000
```
2. **Account for Timezone Day Boundaries**: AdMob reports in Pacific Time (`UTC-8`) by default; App Store Connect reports in UTC. Standardize all incoming dates to UTC before joining.
3. **Audit Invalid Traffic Deductions**: Review AdMob's end-of-month adjustments. If invalid click deductions exceed 3%, investigate fraudulent background ad loops.
