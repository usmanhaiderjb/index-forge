## Step 1: Forensic Triage (Proving Fraud to Apple & Google)

Apple and Google will **NOT** delete a review simply because a user is unhappy with your app or pricing.

To get reviews purged, you must prove that the reviews violate specific platform developer policies:

| Review Bombing Pattern | Identifiable Forensic Evidence | Applicable Platform Policy Violation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Coordinated Bot Attack** | Hundreds of reviews posted from brand new accounts within a 2-hour window sharing identical or scrambled phrasing. | *Spam, Fake Reviews & Manipulation Policy* |
| **Off-Topic / Political Brigade** | Reviews protesting external corporate news, political stances, or social media drama unrelated to app functionality. | *Off-Topic Content & Harassment Guidelines* |
| **Profanity & Hate Speech** | Vulgar language, personal developer threats, or hate speech in review bodies. | *Abusive Content & Community Guidelines* |
| **Competitor Sabotage** | Reviews explicitly promoting a direct competitor app by name in the text (e.g. *"This app sucks, download AppX instead!"*). | *Deceptive Practices & Commercial Promotion* |

```
[Forensic Data Capture Checklist]
├── Date & Timestamp Array of all attack reviews
├── Account Name patterns (e.g. "user9812", "user9813", "randomString")
├── Exact text duplicates across multiple reviewer IDs
└── Zero in-app telemetry match (proving reviewer never actually opened the binary)
```

## Step 2: How to File Removal Disputes

### On the Apple App Store (App Store Connect)

```
[Apple Dispute Workflow]
1. Log in to App Store Connect > My Apps > [Your App] > Ratings and Reviews.
2. Filter reviews by "1 Star" and target date range.
3. Locate the offending review and click "Report a Concern".
4. Select the specific violation category:
   - "It's spam or off-topic"
   - "It's abusive or offensive"
   - "It's a duplicate of another review"
5. Write a concise, objective explanation referencing Apple App Store Review Guideline Section 5.6.
```

If you are facing a massive coordinated attack with hundreds of reviews:
* Do NOT report 500 reviews one by one through the web UI.
* Open an urgent case directly with **Apple Developer Technical Support**:
  * Navigate to `developer.apple.com/contact`.
  * Select **App Store Promotion & Reviews > Inaccurate Ratings & Review Attacks**.
  * Attach a CSV export containing the Review IDs, Timestamps, and pattern analysis.
  * Apple's anti-fraud engineering team can batch-purge bot waves across entire storefronts.

### On Google Play Console

```
[Google Play Dispute Workflow]
1. Open Google Play Console > Quality > Ratings and reviews > Reviews.
2. Select the flag icon beside the fraudulent review.
3. Choose "Inappropriate content" or "Spam".
4. For large-scale attacks, submit an appeal via the Google Play Review Dispute Form at:
   https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/contact/review_removal
5. Provide package name, date spikes, and evidence of policy violation.
```

## Step 3: The Rating Counter-Offensive

While Apple and Google investigate your dispute (which takes 3 to 7 business days), you must protect your rolling average rating from collapsing below the critical **4.0-star threshold**:

```
[Rating Recovery Formula]
5-Star Reviews Needed = [Target (4.5) x (Current Count + N) - Current Stars] / (5 - Target (4.5))
```

1. **Activate In-App Review Prompts for High-Engagement Users**:
   * Trigger the native `SKStoreReviewController` (iOS) or `ReviewManager` (Android) immediately after a high-satisfaction user milestone (e.g. completing a 7-day streak or exporting a project).
   * Prompting satisfied active users generates a surge of 5-star reviews that dilutes the attack volume within 48 hours.
2. **Deploy an Empathetic Developer Reply**:
   * Post a calm, professional response acknowledging the issue:
   * *"We are aware of an active bot attack targeting our app today. Our team is actively investigating with Apple/Google. If you are a genuine user experiencing an issue, please email our founders directly at support@indexforge.com."*
   * This immediately disarms prospective users who see the 1-star reviews.
