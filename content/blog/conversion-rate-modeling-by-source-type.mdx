1. **Establish Baseline CVR Metrics**: Segment conversion performance by traffic source (Search, Browse, App Referral).

3. **Calculate Required Sample Sizes**: Ensure testing runs until achieving minimum statistical power (typically 2,000+ installs/variant).
4. **Automate Regional Rollouts**: Deploy winning variants across localized storefronts while respecting cultural nuances.
