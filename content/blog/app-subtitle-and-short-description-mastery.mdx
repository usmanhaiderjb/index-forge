Along with your App Title, the **Subtitle on iOS (30 characters)** and the **Short Description on Google Play (80 characters)** are the most critical pieces of text metadata you control.

They serve two simultaneous, competing masters:
1. **The Search Algorithm**: They carry massive algorithmic indexing weight for keyword discovery.
2. **The Human User**: They appear directly in search results before the user taps into your page, serving as your primary value proposition hook.

Crafting a subtitle that fits high-volume keywords, conveys a compelling benefit, and lands at **exactly 29 or 30 characters without exceeding the limit** is a precise engineering discipline.

In this guide, we break down high-converting copywriting formulas, common character-limit rejections, and how to balance keyword density with human readability.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               SUBTITLE (iOS) vs SHORT DESCRIPTION (Google Play)                  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  Store Dimension           iOS Subtitle                Google Play Short Desc     |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  Character Limit           30 characters (Strict)      80 characters (Strict)     |
|  Indexing Weight           High (Second only to Title) Highest text signal on Play|
|  SERP Visibility           Visible under App Title     Visible in search & cards  |
|  Permitted Characters      Letters, numbers, symbols   Plain text (No HTML tags)  |
|  Rejection Risk            Claims like "#1 app", "Best" Claims like "Free", emoji |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## The 3 High-Converting Formulas for iOS Subtitles (30 Chars)

With only 30 characters, every single keystroke (including spaces) must pull its weight:

```
[30-Character Budget Breakdown]
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ "S c a n ,   S i g n   &   S h a r e   P D F s"│  --> Exact Length: 29 / 30 Characters
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  ├── Action Verbs (High Search Volume): "Scan", "Sign", "Share"
  └── Core Entity Keyword: "PDFs"
```

### Formula 1: The 3-Verb Action Hook `[Verb], [Verb] & [Verb] [Keyword]`
* *Example 1*: `Scan, Sign & Share PDFs` (23 chars)
* *Example 2*: `Track, Plan & Split Bills` (25 chars)
* *Example 3*: `Log, Track & Burn Calories` (26 chars)
* **Why it works**: Action verbs match direct search queries while creating immediate clarity on what the app does.

### Formula 2: The Core Problem + Outcome `[Primary Keyword] for [Outcome]`
* *Example 1*: `Expense Tracker for Couples` (27 chars)
* *Example 2*: `Fast Intermittent Timer` (23 chars)
* *Example 3*: `HIIT Interval Workout Timer` (27 chars)
* **Why it works**: Directly targets long-tail persona queries where users search for their specific use-case.

### Formula 3: The Feature Qualifier `[Feature Modifier] [Target Keyword]`
* *Example 1*: `Private & Offline Expense Log` (29 chars)
* *Example 2*: `Simple Daily Habit Planner` (26 chars)
* *Example 3*: `HD Document & Receipt Scanner` (29 chars)

## Google Play Short Description Architecture (80 Chars)

On Google Play, the Short Description is expanded to **80 characters**. Because Google's search engine indexes natural language sentences, the short description should read as a clean, grammatically complete value proposition:

```
[80-Character Budget Breakdown]
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ "Track daily expenses, manage family budgets, and save money with smart tools" │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  --> Length: 77 / 80 Characters. Rich in 5 semantic entities.
```

### 3 High-Converting Google Play Short Description Patterns:

1. **The Comprehensive Benefit Stack (75–80 chars)**:
   * `"Manage personal finances, track daily spending, and achieve your savings goals."` (78 chars)
2. **The Problem/Solution Value Proposition (72–80 chars)**:
   * `"Fast, private PDF document scanner with OCR text recognition and cloud export."` (79 chars)
3. **The Multi-Feature Workflow Summary (70–78 chars)**:
   * `"Simple interval timer for HIIT, Tabata, gym workouts, and home circuit training."` (78 chars)

## 4 Policy Triggers That Cause Metadata Rejections

Both Apple App Review and Google Play automated compliance bots will reject your update if your Subtitle or Short Description contains:

| Policy Violation | Rejection Example | Compliant Alternative |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Unsubstantiated Rank Claims** | `"The #1 Fasting App in the US"` | `"Fast & Simple Fasting Timer"` |
| **Pricing Claims in Metadata** | `"Free Expense Tracker App"` | `"Personal Expense Tracker"` |
| **Store Metadata References** | `"Best App of 2026 on App Store"`| `"Award-Winning Habit Tracker"` |
| **Excessive Emoji / Symbols** | `"🔥 Track Habits & Win! ⭐️"` | `"Track Habits & Daily Routines"`|
